Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

Simple answers so you can book with confidence.

What happens after I book?

Your request opens WhatsApp with High Stars. The barber confirms your appointment directly with you.

Is there a deposit?

There is no online deposit in this current website version. If High Stars introduces a deposit policy, it can be added here.

How soon can we talk?

Call or WhatsApp +260 97 5559060 directly.

Where is High Stars located?

Mtendere East, Lusaka, Zambia. The exact Google Maps location is available on the Contact page.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are subject to availability. Booking ahead is recommended.

Can I request a specific style?

Yes. Mention the style in WhatsApp and, where useful, send a reference photo.
