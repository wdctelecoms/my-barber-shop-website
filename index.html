Questions answered

Before you book.

What happens after I book?

Your request goes directly to the owner. We'll contact you on WhatsApp or phone to confirm the time and appointment.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

For this review version, no online deposit is required. Any deposit policy can be added once the barber confirms the preferred payment method.

How soon can we talk?

Because the booking goes directly to the owner, you can communicate without a sales team or middleman.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins can be accepted subject to availability. Booking ahead is recommended to secure your preferred time.
